# Specification-Rich Sovereignty Frameworks — Provenance Note

Checked on: 2026-04-23

## Purpose

This note records the boundary for the generalized
`specification-rich-sovereignty-frameworks` case study.

The case is intentionally framed as a reusable governance pattern rather than
as a project-specific critique.

## Public Source Basis

The primary trigger is a public repository:

- [Euro-Cloud-Substrate](https://github.com/kevin-biot/Euro-Cloud-Substrate)

Within that public source base, the most useful materials are the public
mappings and governance/conformance documents, including:

- public sovereignty-framework crosswalks such as
  `docs/mappings/es3-mapping.md`
- public crosswalks to the Commission Cloud Sovereignty Framework such as
  `docs/mappings/ec-cloud-sovereignty-framework-mapping.md`
- governance and conformance documents such as `GOVERNANCE.md`,
  `docs/conformance/model.md`, and invariant/profile notes

Those public materials show a visible and reusable shape:

- strong invariant language
- explicit authority-before-execution and refusal semantics
- evidence-driven conformance intent
- anti-capture guardrails
- a more mature technical doctrine than institutional conformance authority

That shape is sufficient to motivate the generalized governance question:

- what happens when architectural doctrine matures faster than adjudication and
  market-claim discipline?

## Claim posture

The current case is best understood as:

- mostly `source-backed`
- partly `analytic inference`

It is no longer best described as primarily private-internal provisional.
