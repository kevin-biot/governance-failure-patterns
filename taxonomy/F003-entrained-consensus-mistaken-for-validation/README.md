# F003 — Entrained Consensus Mistaken for Validation

## Claim

Agreement inside a shared reasoning environment can be mistaken for independent
validation when it is actually produced by coupling, shared priors, or common
constraints.

## Mechanism

Participants share models, prompts, source packs, institutional language, or
iterative drafts. Their convergence is then structurally dependent rather than
epistemically independent.

## Observable Signature

- rapid convergence after iterative exposure to shared drafts
- low diversity of alternative framings
- high confidence attached to internal agreement

## False Reassurance Pattern

Consensus is treated as confirmation, even though the participants are
effectively variations of the same constrained system.

## Minimal Assumptions

- strong context sharing
- iterative exposure to the same artifacts
- weak independence between contributors

## Where It Does Not Apply

It does not apply equally where raters or analysts are genuinely independent in
training, context, incentives, and source material.

## Mitigations

- counter-frame analysis
- independence tests
- explicit diversity of source packs
- deterministic measurement layer beneath deliberation
