# F001 — Formal Transparency Without Foundational Adequacy

## Claim

A system can be explicit and reproducible about how it processes inputs while
remaining weak or contestable about the premises that made those inputs
admissible in the first place.

## Mechanism

Normative choices are translated into criteria, thresholds, and weights before
the formal method begins. The resulting process looks rigorous at the level of
calculation, which can hide fragility at the level of framing.

## Observable Signature

- detailed methods section and clean scoring logic
- weak or absent justification for criteria construction
- little visibility into excluded stakeholders, omitted harms, or suppressed
  horizons

## False Reassurance Pattern

The system appears legitimate because the mechanics are transparent, even
though the premises remain under-justified.

## Minimal Assumptions

- a formal scoring or decision-analytic layer exists
- upstream framing choices are not equally governed
- audiences conflate process clarity with justification

## Where It Does Not Apply

This class does not claim that all formal methods are invalid. It applies when
mechanical transparency outpaces constitutional or normative adequacy.

## Mitigations

- normative charter
- stakeholder admissibility register
- omission register
- counter-frame requirement
