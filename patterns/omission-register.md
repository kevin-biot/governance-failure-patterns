# Pattern — Omission Register

## Purpose

An omission register makes visible what a framework cannot or does not
represent well.

## Core moves

- list excluded stakeholders, values, or factors
- state why they were omitted
- distinguish deliberate omission from unresolved uncertainty
- publish omissions alongside outputs, not after criticism arrives

## What this prevents

- hidden blind spots presented as neutral scope choices
- summary outputs that conceal what was flattened or excluded
- weaker forms of `F001` Formal Transparency Without Foundational Adequacy

## Minimal artefacts

- omitted element
- reason for omission
- expected consequence of omission
- review date or challenge path

## Working question

What matters here but is not represented directly in the framework?

## Boundaries

An omission register does not solve exclusion by itself. It prevents omission
from becoming invisible.
